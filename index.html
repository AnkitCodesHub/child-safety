Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Consnow is committed to the safety and protection of all users, especially minors. This page outlines our standards and practices against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Age Requirement

Consnow is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts or use our services. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will promptly delete that account and all associated data.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE

Consnow has a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content and behavior. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or endangers children
  • Grooming behavior directed at minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or soliciting child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any attempt to contact minors for inappropriate purposes

3. Prevention Measures

  • Consnow uses a mutual consent connection model — both users must exchange a unique code to connect. There is no public discovery, search, or messaging feature that could be used to contact strangers or minors.
  • The app does not include any public profiles, feeds, or content sharing features.
  • Users can only see the activity status of people they have explicitly chosen to connect with.

4. Reporting

If you encounter any behavior or content that you believe involves the exploitation of a child, please report it immediately to:

  • Email: ankitraj5309@gmail.com
  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC): www.missingkids.org
  • Your local law enforcement agency

We will investigate all reports promptly and take appropriate action, including removing content, disabling accounts, and reporting to relevant authorities as required by law.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Consnow will fully cooperate with law enforcement agencies in the investigation of any suspected child exploitation. We will comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the reporting and handling of CSAE material.

6. Account Actions

Users found to be in violation of these standards will have their accounts immediately and permanently terminated. We reserve the right to report any such violations to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.
